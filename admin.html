NOTICES

Shown once to each writer, the next time they open the app between the start and end times. Offers can be turned off by writers; updates and competition news can't. Don't put anything secret in a scheduled notice: it's stored before it starts showing.

COMPETITIONS

Writers only see a competition (menu link, dashboard banner, competition page) while it's open. Drafts are seen by no one. The theme stays locked until opening time, even for a published competition. All times are UK time.

Bosses: mossy golem (halfway), horned demon (final). Buddies: Tiny Draft the duckling (Finisher), Deadline Hopkins the hare (Entrant), Doe Dénouement the deer (Shortlisted), Reynard Redraft the fox (Champion). These are the launch set; later competitions will need new art.

SUSPENDED WRITERS

Suspended writers can sign in, read and export their writing, but can't add to it or use friends, messages, groups or sharing.
